3. Cross-section reasoning (hard — tests retrieval, not just one chunk)

→ Expect a synthesized answer pulling from section 2 (lithium-ion) and section 6 (hydrogen), plus the reasoning in section 7. This checks whether your pipeline can retrieve and combine multiple non-adjacent chunks correctly.

4. Distractor test (checks for hallucination/false retrieval)
"What is the round-trip efficiency of CAES paired with concentrated solar power?"
→ This is a trick — CAES and molten salt/concentrated solar are two different sections (4 and 5). A good pipeline should either say the document doesn't combine those two, or correctly cite each separately rather than blending numbers from both sections into a false combined answer.
